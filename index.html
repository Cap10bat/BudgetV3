Etheridge Household Finance System

❓ Help Center

🏠 Dashboard

Dashboard provides a quick overview of current finances and answers the question: Where do we stand right now?

📌 Coming Up

Displays unpaid bills and unreceived income expected during the next 30 days.

💰 Paychecks

Shows all scheduled paychecks for the year. Marking income as received updates forecasts, balances and reports.

📈 Forecasts

NOW shows current reality. PROJECTED assumes future income and bills occur as planned.

📊 Monthly Summary

Provides a quick month status showing received income, paid bills and remaining activity.

📅 Timeline

Displays upcoming future financial events in chronological order.

📖 Ledger

Permanent historical record of completed income and expense activity.

🎯 Goals

Goals are informational only. Goals never affect forecasting calculations.

✅ Reconcile

Update actual account balances and recalculate all financial data.

📜 Activity Log

Automatically records major events including bills paid, income received, imports, exports and balance updates.

🔄 Sync

Imports merge newer changes into existing data. Imports never replace the entire budget.
